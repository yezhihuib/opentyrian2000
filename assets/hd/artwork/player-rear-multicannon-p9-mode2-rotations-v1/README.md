# Rear Multi-Cannon P9 mode2: exact steep twin transforms

Resources298/299 retain the approved warm energy colors, antialiased soft edges,
rounded luminous leading caps and narrowing tails of generated277. Both source
and target retain the logical12×14 canvas and anchor(0,0). Source277 is the
accepted P5 mode2 lower-over-upper assembly of the generated281 vector master.

An independent authentic source check established exact equality of all168
INDEX8 and coverage cells under these maps, with no covered cell clipped:

-298: x'=y, y'=13−x at logical density1
-299: x'=11−y, y'=13−x at logical density1

At authored densityd the maps are x'=y, y'=14d−1−x for298 and
x'=12d−1−y, y'=14d−1−x for299. The outputs are exact full-canvas horizontal
reflections. Only transparent-black samples lie outside the retained canvas.
The nonzero bounds are[0,3,10,14] for298 and[2,3,12,14] for299.

This is a lossless coordinate conversion, with no drawing, recoloring,
resampling, filtering, alpha adjustment or brightness compensation. Every
supported straight-RGBA sample, the fractional-edge sample count, alpha sum
and premultiplied RGB energy are preserved exactly. Density4 and density8
use their corresponding accepted sources separately; neither is resampled.
The generated-only builder requires no original sprite, mask, palette or
capture. Exact original-source verification does not imply that smooth HD art
traces original pixel boundaries.

Run python3 build.py --output /absolute/fresh/output. Input hashes must match
inputs.json; output destinations must be fresh. The deterministic RGBA PNG
codec uses stored DEFLATE blocks, independent of library versions. Equivalent
straightRGBA, PNG and exact-EOF HDR1 files use role131 keys1310298/1310299.
The output receipt records all hashes, pins, geometry and conservation checks.

The comparison previews show exact native2x/4x center-nearest samples on
synthetic dark/brown/cloud backgrounds. Their pixels are not enlarged after
sampling. Native sampling is applied after the full-density transform; its
phase means transforming a previously sampled image would differ. The
previews establish artwork appearance only, not game or runtime ownership.
No original game data or captures are included in this source package.
